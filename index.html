MoodFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Version: 1.1.0

MoodFlow ("we," "our," or "us") is a mood tracker for iOS, developed by SudarshanTechLabs. MoodFlow is a mood tracker with coping strategies and optional AI insights. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using MoodFlow you agree to the practices described below.

Overview

MoodFlow is designed to be private by default. Your data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate accounts, servers, or databases that hold your personal information, and we cannot see the content you create in the app. The one exception is optional AI weekly insights, which send your recent mood entries to Google Gemini when you request them (see Third-Party Services).

Information We Collect

Location Data

  • MoodFlow does not collect location data.

Account Information

  • No account is required. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • MoodFlow contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions, so no billing information is processed.

Device Information

  • Content you create in the app is stored locally on your device. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics events.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the features you use on your device. The one exception is AI weekly insights, which send mood entries to Google Gemini when you tap Generate weekly insights.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone, except sending mood entries to Google Gemini when you request AI weekly insights.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps or websites.

Storage and Retention

Data Storage

  • Your content is stored locally using Apple's standard on-device storage.
  • Mood entries sent for AI weekly insights are processed by Google under its terms. MoodFlow keeps no copy on any server.
  • If you have iCloud backup enabled for your device, your app data may be included in your own encrypted Apple backup, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • Local data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Deleting an entry in MoodFlow does not delete data already sent to Google Gemini; any copy Google keeps is governed by its terms.

Data Security

  • Data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and the device security controls you enable (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID).
  • Any network communication that does occur uses HTTPS/TLS.

Data Sharing

Third Parties

  • Google Gemini API (only when you tap Generate weekly insights): receives the emotion, intensity, trigger tags, and full note text of each entry from the past 7 days

We do not share your data with:

  • Data brokers
  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics or tracking providers
  • Any third party unrelated to a feature you actively use

Third-Party Services

MoodFlow includes one optional AI feature: AI weekly insights. When, and only when, you tap Generate weekly insights on the Insights screen (at least 3 entries from the past 7 days are needed), MoodFlow sends every mood entry from the past 7 days to Google's Gemini API over HTTPS to generate a weekly trend and suggestions. For each entry it sends the emotion, the intensity (1-10), your trigger tags, and the full text of your note. Entry dates are not sent. Mood notes can contain sensitive personal or health information, so use this feature only if you are comfortable sharing those entries with Google. That data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. No data is sent to Google unless you tap Generate weekly insights.

MoodFlow uses no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services.

Please review the relevant third-party terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service

Permissions

Notifications (optional)

Used to deliver local reminders and alerts you schedule inside the app. Notifications are generated on your device by iOS; no notification content is sent to us or any server. You can turn them off any time in iOS Settings -> MoodFlow -> Notifications.

Your Rights and Controls

Data Access

  • You can view, edit, or delete your content directly inside the app.

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

Because MoodFlow keeps your data on your device and we hold no copy, you already control it directly. If you are in the EU you retain the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing. Since we do not collect or store your data, exercising these rights is done on your own device.

Permissions

  • You can review or revoke permissions any time in iOS Settings -> MoodFlow.
  • Revoking a permission may disable the related optional feature.

Children's Privacy

  • MoodFlow is not intended for children under 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
  • Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of apps that store personal content.

Data Deletion

MoodFlow does not require an account and stores your data on your device. Only AI weekly insight requests leave the device, as described above.

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open MoodFlow
  2. Delete individual entries or content where the app provides controls
  3. Uninstalling the app removes all local app-private data

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to sunny.sudarshan@gmail.com (or support@sudarshantechlabs.com)

  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request - MoodFlow"
  • We will respond, typically within 48 hours

What Gets Deleted

  • All local app data stored by the app on your device

Data Retention After Deletion

  • Immediate: deleting content in-app removes it from your device
  • Uninstall: removes all app-private local data

About MoodFlow

MoodFlow is built with privacy in mind, using:

  • Swift and SwiftUI
  • On-device storage for your data
  • Google Gemini for optional, user-initiated AI assistance

The app collects only what is needed for the features described in this policy, and keeps your data on your device except for the AI weekly insight requests described above.
